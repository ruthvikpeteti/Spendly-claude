Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide Spendly, we collect the information you give us directly:

  • account details, such as your name, email address and a hashed password;
  • expense records you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and notes.

We do not collect payment card details or connect to your bank accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run the service: to sign you in, store your expenses, and show you summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database on the server hosting Spendly. Passwords are stored as hashes, never as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can delete your expenses at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account and all associated data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not share your account or expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
